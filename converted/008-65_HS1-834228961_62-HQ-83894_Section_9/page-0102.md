INTERPLANETARY INTELLIGENCE
FLYING SAUCER OBJECTS
OKLAHOMA CITY, OKLAHOMA

August 24, 1959

United States Department of Justice
Federal Bureau of Investigation
Washington, D.C.
ATTENTION: J. Edgar Hoover

Dear Mr. Hoover:

The purpose of our organization is to investigate (Unidentified Flying Objects) - or saucer reports.
We are planning to publish a publication on the same. (I hope) some time in the near future.
I was wondering if you would like to write an article for the publication on the saucer reports and about 225 words.
Thank you, and hope to hear from you in the near future.
