UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS

At this time there appears to be no necessity for additional instructions for the field of information in the [illegible] manual relative to flying saucers.

10-3-58 points out that our policy concerning unidentified flying objects is to conduct no investigation but immediately to refer reports to the Air Force. (See SAC Letter 58-52, 11-22-58, and SAC Letter 60-1, 1-4-60.) If it is obtained when complaints received by the FBI, that such reports should be noted and the development of any interest in the field of unidentified flying objects be referred to the field of information in the manual of instructions of our policy relative to flying saucers.

During the past twelve months only one complaint concerning flying saucers has been referred to the Air Force, and it was turned over to the [illegible] field.
