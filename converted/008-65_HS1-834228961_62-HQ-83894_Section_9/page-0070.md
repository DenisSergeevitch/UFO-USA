# 65_HS1-834228961_62-HQ-83894_Section_9 - Page 70

Office Memorandum

UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

DATE: December 9, 1950

TO: SAC, Oklahoma City

FROM: [illegible] (Oklahoma City)

SUBJECT: JAMES LAMAR REMAL

Reference is made to Oklahoma City letter to Bureau dated 11/28/50.

On 12/8/50, JAMES LAMAR REMAL was interviewed at Oklahoma City. Remal was advised of the investigation concerning unidentified flying objects and advised that he did not possess any information concerning same.

JAMES LAMAR REMAL, who was advised of the source of the report, advised that he had not seen the UFO and had not discussed same with anyone. He advised that he had no knowledge of the source of the information and could not give any information which would be of value to the Bureau. An examination of the files of the Oklahoma City Office failed to reveal any information concerning Remal. It is noted that the publication "TRUE" magazine, which contained the article concerning the UFOs, was received from a friend, and Remal had borrowed the publication from him. Remal stated that he had no further information to offer.

[Stamp: MR. JONES]
