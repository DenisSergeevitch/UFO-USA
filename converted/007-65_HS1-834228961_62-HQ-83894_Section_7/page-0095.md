# 65_HS1-834228961_62-HQ-83894_Section_7 - Page 95

Office Memorandum - UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO: M. A. BELMONT
FROM: J. A. C[illegible]
DATE: OCTOBER 28, 1952
SUBJECT: FLYING SAUCERS

Air Intelligence advised of another credible report concerning the sighting of flying saucers. Air Intelligence advised that this report is being analyzed and that they are considering the possibility of [illegible].

Air Intelligence advised that they do not have the human resources available to conduct a full research on the flying saucer problem. Air Intelligence advised that they are interested in the Technical Intelligence reports on the sightings and the motion and appearance of the objects to determine if they are identifiable objects or if the sightings have been unexplained.

Colonel [illegible] of Intelligence, U.S. Air Force, advised that he would like to have the FBI's assistance in this matter. A request has been made that the FBI assist in the investigation of the flying saucer reports by having the FBI agents in the field report on the sightings of the objects in the areas where they are reported. Colonel [illegible] advised that the Air Intelligence would like to have the Technical Intelligence reports on the sightings submitted to them.
