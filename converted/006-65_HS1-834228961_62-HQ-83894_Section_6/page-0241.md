Of flying Discs
F.B.I. Wash. D.C.
Gentlemen: Dear Sirs.

I have a theory on the so called
flying saucers.
I don't know where to write to express
my opinion.
But yours is the best office in the land
[Handwritten insertion over "in the land"]: W. H. Jennings
when it comes to investigations.

If and when I get ready to commit a crime
I will expect to get caught but so be it.
If the flying saucers are real then
in my opinion they are not from another
planet. I would be willing to bet
they are the brain child of General
Mossbang
