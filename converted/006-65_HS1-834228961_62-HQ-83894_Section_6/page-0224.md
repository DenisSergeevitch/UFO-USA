# 65_HS1-834228961_62-HQ-83894_Section_6 - Page 224

Office Memorandum - UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

DATE: August 6, 1952

TO: Director, FBI
FROM: SAC, Cincinnati (62-0)
SUBJECT: ANONYMOUS COMMUNICATION WRITTEN IN CINCINNATI, OHIO RECEIVED BY CINCINNATI "ENQUIRER" RE: "FLYING SAUCERS" - SECURITY MATTER - X

M. R. [illegible] of the staff of the "Cincinnati Enquirer" furnished the Cincinnati Office with the enclosed letter and envelope postmarked 8-5-52 at Cincinnati, Ohio.

It will be noted that the letter is written in the same language as the enclosed letter translation of the "Cincinnati Enquirer".

In view of the widespread interest in so-called "flying saucers", I am forwarding this material to the Bureau with the thought that the Bureau may desire to have a translation made of the attached letter and the results made available to other Government agencies.

Enclosures (3)

WNW:MI
