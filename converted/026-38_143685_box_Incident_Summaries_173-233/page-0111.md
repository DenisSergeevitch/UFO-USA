# 38_143685_box_Incident_Summaries_173-233 - Page 111

Incident: 220

Mr Robert Thatcher, an AF Veteran, now a Science teacher at the Francisco Jr High School, called Hq 4th AF to report a yellow light flash across the sky at approximately 2113 hours the night of 29 November 1948. Light was headed due North then turned North-Northwest and disappeared. It lasted for about 7 seconds and appeared to be traveling too fast for a plane. However, it did not appear to be a comet or shooting star.
